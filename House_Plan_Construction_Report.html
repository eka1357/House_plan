Architectural Construction Report

2-Story Residential Building (25 ft × 60 ft = 1500 sq ft Plot)

Location: Tirupati, Andhra Pradesh Total Built-Up Area: 3,000 sq. ft. Facing: East Facing

1. Executive Summary & Architectural Overview

This document details the complete step-by-step 3D BIM modeling and structural construction process for a 2-story residential building located in Tirupati, Andhra Pradesh. The project is designed on a 25 ft × 60 ft (1,500 sq. ft.) East-facing plot with a total built-up area of 3,000 sq. ft. (Ground Floor: 1,500 sq. ft. + First Floor: 1,500 sq. ft.).

  • Ground Floor: Open Car Parking bay at East entrance, spacious Living Hall, Open Kitchen with counter partition, Master Bedroom (MB) with attached bath alcove, and Child Bedroom (CB) with attached bath alcove.
  • First Floor: Independent rental/residence unit featuring 3 Bedrooms (B1, B2, B3), Kitchen (Kit), Living Hall, and direct external access.
  • External Access: External dog-legged staircase running along the South setback wall providing independent access to the 1st floor without entering the ground floor.

2. Comprehensive Step-by-Step Construction Methodology

Step 1: Substructure & Foundation Slab

Specifications: 60 ft × 25 ft × 6 inches RCC foundation slab sitting below ground level (Z = -0.5 ft to 0.0 ft). Designed to distribute structural dead load and live load evenly across the Tirupati regional soil stratum.

Step 2: Ground Floor Exterior Walls & Gate Opening

Specifications: 9-inch thick load-bearing exterior brickwork (Z = 0.0 ft to 10.0 ft). Features a wide 16 ft × 8 ft vehicular gate opening on the front East wall for car entry.

Step 3: Ground Floor Interior Partition Walls

Specifications: 4.5-inch thick interior partition walls (Height = 10 ft). Configured for an open kitchen concept with L-shaped counter partition wall, Master Bedroom, Child Bedroom, and wardrobe alcoves.

Step 4: Ground Floor Door & Window Openings

Specifications: Precise boolean cutouts for doors (Main Entrance: 4×7 ft, MB/CB/Kitchen: 3×7 ft) and windows (Living: 6×4 ft, Bedrooms: 5×4 ft, Kitchen: 4×3 ft, Bathroom: 2×2 ft ventilator).

Step 5: External Dog-Legged Staircase

Specifications: Positioned OUTSIDE along the South exterior wall. Flight width = 3.25 ft, 16 risers @ 7.5 in, 10 in treads, mid-landing at Z = 5.0 ft, climbing seamlessly to 1st floor level at Z = 10.0 ft.

Step 6: First Floor Slab & Staircase Landing Arrival

Specifications: 6-inch RCC floor slab (Z = 9.5 ft to 10.0 ft) with a 6 ft × 3 ft cutout at the South wall for staircase arrival access.

Step 7: First Floor Superstructure & Room Partitioning

Specifications: 9-inch exterior walls and 4.5-inch interior partition walls (Z = 10.0 ft to 20.0 ft) forming Bedroom 1 (B1), Bedroom 2 (B2), Bedroom 3 (B3), Kitchen, and Hall.

Step 8: First Floor Doors & Window Openings

Specifications: Individual accessible doorways for B1, B2, B3, and Kitchen, plus 8 exterior window cutouts ensuring natural light and ventilation in every room.

Step 9: Roof Slab & Parapet Safety Walls

Specifications: 6-inch reinforced concrete roof slab at Z = 20.0 ft to 20.5 ft, topped with a 3 ft high, 4.5 in thick perimeter parapet safety wall (Z = 20.5 ft to 23.5 ft).

Step 10: Exterior Details & Finishes

Specifications: Front entrance plinth access steps, 3-inch projecting window sills, main door frame, and aluminum roof edge fascia trim.

Step 11: Realistic Materials & Shading

Specifications: White painted exterior plaster, matte white interior paint, concrete slab finish, light grey vitrified floor tiles, rich teak wood doors, and black metallic aluminum + clear glass windows.

Step 12: Daylight & Interior Lighting Setup

Specifications: Warm Sun daylight (4.5W, 3° soft shadow angle) + Sky blue environment ambient fill + 9 interior warm tungsten area lights (35W–60W).

5. Brick & Block Comparison Analysis for Tirupati Region

RECOMMENDED CHOICE

Autoclaved Aerated Concrete (AAC) Blocks

Size: 600 mm × 200 mm × 150/200 mm (Equivalent to 6–8 traditional red bricks).

Cost in Tirupati: ~₹60 to ₹70 per block (~₹50 to ₹55 per cu. ft.).

  • Thermal Insulation: AAC blocks have 3× superior thermal insulation compared to red clay bricks. In Tirupati's intense summer climate (where temperatures cross 40°C), AAC blocks keep interior rooms 3°C to 5°C cooler, reducing AC electricity bills by 25–30%.
  • Weight Reduction: 50% lighter than red clay bricks, significantly reducing structural dead load on columns and footings.
  • Labor & Mortar Savings: Larger block size speeds up wall masonry work by 30% and uses thin-bed joint adhesive instead of thick cement mortar.
  • Earthquake Resistance: Light weight provides better seismic performance.

Verdict: AAC Blocks are the BEST, MOST DURABLE, and MOST ECONOMICAL choice for construction in Tirupati.
